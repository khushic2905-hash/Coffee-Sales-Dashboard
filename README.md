# Coffee-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive dashboard analyzing coffee sales performance, key business insights using IBM Cognos Analytics.


## Objectives
- Analyze overall coffee sales performance.
- Identify the most popular coffee products.
- Understand sales trends over time.
- Compare sales across different coffee types.
- Identify key patterns and insights from the sales data.

## Dataset
- The dataset used for this project is the Coffee Sales dataset available on Kaggle.
- Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ihelon/coffee-sales
- The dataset is released under the CC0: Public Domain license.

## Dashboard
### The dashboard provides visual insights into:
- Total Money(Sum)
- Average Money
- Money By date
- Money by Cash type
- Money by Cash type for different coffee types

## Key Insights
- Total Money Generated from sales of coffee: 115,432. Money has a weak upward trend.
- Average Money is $ 31.75. Money has a weak downward trend.
- <img width="954" height="259" alt="Sum and Average of money" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a0571840-221e-4230-a452-3c12dfeacba2" />

- Date 2024-10-11 has the highest money at 836.66, followed by 2025-02-03 at 796.0.
- from 2025-03-22 to 2025-03-23, money dropped by 44%.
- Across all dates, the sum of money is over 115 thousand.
- Money ranges from 23.02, when date is 2024-08-29 to 836.7 when date is 2024-10-11.

### Money by cash type colored by coffee name chart:
- For money, the most significant values of coffee name are latte and americano with milk, whose respective money values add up to over 53 thousand, or 46% of the total.
- Money is most unusual when coffee name is latte, Espresso and Americano with Milk.
- For money, the most significant value of cash type is card whose respective money values add up to over 112 thousand or 97.2% of the total.

### money by cash type chart:
- Mostly payment are done using card. card exceeds cash in money by 97.2%.
- From 2024-10-11 to 2024-10-12, Cash type card's money dropped by 74%.
- From 2025-03-22 to 2025-03-23, Card's money dropped by 44%.
